Running — Phase Breakdown

Phase 1 — Walk/Run (Wks 1–2)
  • Week 1: Walk 90s / Jog 3 min ×6 (~27 min)
  • Week 2: Walk 90s / Jog 5 min ×4 (~26 min)
  • Jog at fully conversational pace
  • 5 min brisk walk warm-up before every run
  • XC background: resist going faster than conversational
Phase 2 — Continuous (Wks 3–6)
  • Week 3: 20 min continuous
  • Week 4: 23 min continuous
  • Week 5: 26 min continuous
  • Week 6: 30 min ★ Milestone
  • No walk breaks — slow the pace instead
Phase 3 — Long Run Build (Wks 7–12)
  • Mon/Wed: 30–35 min easy runs
  • Saturday = Long Run (builds weekly)
  • Wk 7: 35 → Wk 9: 45 → Wk 12: 60 min ★
  • Long run pace = slower than easy runs

Gym Workout — Upper Body Machines

Weight: Start light. Last 2–3 reps of each set should feel challenging but form stays clean. Rest 60–90 sec between sets. For video form demos, search each exercise name on YouTube (Jeff Nippard or Athlean-X are great).

Gym Progression

  • Weeks 1–4: Learn the machines. Stay lighter, focus on form.
  • Weeks 5–8: Add 5 lbs to any machine that feels easy all 3 sets.
  • Weeks 9–12: Continue adding weight. Try a 4th set on your strongest exercises.
  • Never sacrifice form for weight.

Cold Weather (Oct–Nov)

  • Gym is fine year-round — cold doesn't affect it.
  • Running outdoors is what becomes tough: cold, dark, icy, unmotivating.
  • Too cold/icy outside? Use the treadmill — same plan, same time.
  • The 2nd gym day per week (added Week 7) gives you an active day if a run gets skipped.

Pre/Post Run Routine

Warm-Up (5 min before)

  1. 2 min brisk walk
  2. Leg swings forward & back — 10 each leg
  3. Leg swings side to side — 10 each leg
  4. Walking knee raises — 20 steps
  5. Walking butt kicks — 20 steps
  6. Hip circles — 10 each direction

Cool-Down (5 min after)

  1. 2 min easy walk
  2. Standing quad stretch — 30 sec each leg
  3. Calf stretch against wall — 30 sec each
  4. Hip flexor lunge stretch — 30 sec each side
  5. Seated hamstring stretch — 30 sec each leg
  6. Figure-4 / pigeon stretch — 30 sec each side

Nutrition

Protein (Priority #1)

  • Target: 130–140g/day
  • Eggs, chicken, turkey, lean beef
  • Greek yogurt, cottage cheese
  • Protein shake if needed
  • 30–40g per meal

Fueling Workouts

  • Don't run or lift fasted (20+ min sessions)
  • Pre-workout snack 30–45 min before: banana, toast + PB, oatmeal
  • Post-workout: protein + carbs within 60 min

Hydration

  • Target: ~3 liters/day
  • Drink throughout the day, not just at workouts
  • A glass before and after every run
  • Dark urine = drink more

Key Rules

Do These

  • Run at conversational pace — especially weeks 1–6.
  • Get proper running shoes with a gait analysis.
  • Sleep 7–9 hours — that's when your body rebuilds.
  • Three average weeks beats one great week followed by injury.
  • If you miss a day, just pick back up. Don't "make it up."

Avoid These

  • Going too fast — your XC background will make slow feel embarrassingly slow. Trust it.
  • Running through sharp joint or bone pain.
  • Skipping rest days — recovery IS the training.
  • Adding extra workouts in weeks 1–4.
  • Comparing to your high school self.
